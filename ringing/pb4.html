Bellringing on 4 bells

A rotatable 3D view of the 24 rows on four bells, arranged as the permutohedron (combinatorially a truncated octahedron).

The vertices are the rows (for example 1234). The three single adjacent swaps (12), (23), and (34) are exactly the edges of the truncated octahedron. The fourth legal change (12)(34) joins opposite corners of one of its six square faces, so every legal change is now either a polyhedron edge or a face diagonal.

Controls

Drag in the diagram to rotate it. Use the mouse wheel or the +/− keys to zoom. All controls work while an animation is running.

Edge colours

(12) swap the first two places
(23) swap the middle two places
(34) swap the last two places
(12)(34) swap both outer pairs simultaneously

The red (12)(34) lines are the diagonals of the six square faces; all other move lines are actual edges of the solid.

Methods

Plain Hunt alternates x and (23) until rounds, visiting 8 rows. Plain Bob Minimus follows x, (23), x, (23), x, (23), x, (34), repeated until rounds, and visits all 24 rows. The travelling dot marks the current change; each row turns black when it has been visited.

Choose Plain Hunt or Plain Bob Minimus to start.
