Bellringing on 5 bells

The 120 rows are the vertices of the four-dimensional permutohedron, shown here by a fixed projection into 3D. The mouse rotates that 3D projection.

Labelling each geometric vertex by the inverse permutation makes a bellringing change act on adjacent places. The four single swaps are permutohedron edges; the three possible simultaneous disjoint swaps are diagonals of square 2-faces.

Methods

Plain Hunt alternates (12)(34) and (23)(45) and returns to rounds after 10 changes. A plain lead of Plain Bob Doubles has the same hunting changes but ends with (34) instead of the final (23)(45). The plain course is 4 leads = 40 changes.

The 120 shown here uses twelve leads with calls P P P B   P P P B   P P P B; at a bob the lead-end change is (23) instead of (34).
