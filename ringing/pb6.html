Bellringing on 6 bells

The 720 rows are the vertices of the five-dimensional permutohedron. They are first put in their natural 5D hyperplane and then projected into 3D; you can rotate that 3D projection with the mouse.

As on the smaller versions, the geometric vertices are labelled by the inverse permutation. This makes adjacent swaps of places into genuine permutohedron edges. Simultaneous independent swaps appear as diagonals of square faces; (12)(34)(56) is the long diagonal of a cubical 3-face.

Animation

Drag to rotate. Mouse wheel or +/− zooms; 0 restores 100% zoom. Row labels are off initially because there are 720 of them; hover a vertex to see its row.

Methods

Plain Hunt alternates (12)(34)(56) with (23)(45) and returns to rounds after 12 changes. Plain Bob Minor has place notation x16 x16 x16 x16 x16 x12; a plain course is five 12-change leads, hence 60 changes.

The 720 uses ordinary Plain Bob Minor lead-end calls: plain 12, bob 14, single 1234. The 60-lead composition is J. F. Penning's extent, expanded here directly as P/B/S calls.
